Privacy Policy

Gears

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Gears is a puzzle game for iPhone and Android. It works without an account, and we don't collect your name, email or other personal details.

Data that stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, hints), the gears and backgrounds you own, and your settings are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to us.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules local notifications on your phone. No server is involved and nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the game's settings or in your phone's settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; the game only learns which items you bought, to unlock them.

Advertising

The free version of Gears shows ads, provided by a third-party advertising network. To show and measure ads, the network may collect data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Ads are personalised only with your consent: on iPhone the game asks for permission before any tracking, and in regions where it's required you're asked for consent first. You can also limit ad personalisation in your phone's settings. Buying "No ads" removes the ads shown between levels.

Analytics

The game has no analytics or tracking of its own.

Children

Gears is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

All game data is removed when you delete the app. We hold no data about you on our side; for anything the advertising network holds, use the ad settings on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
