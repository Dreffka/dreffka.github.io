Questions

Why do my gears jam?

Neighbouring gears turn opposite ways. If gears close into a ring with an odd number of them (three gears that all touch, for example), they can't all turn, and the whole group stops. Break the ring by moving one gear.

How do I get more hints?

Tap Hint in a level: when you have no hints left, you can watch a short ad for one, or get a hint pack in the Shop.

How do I remove ads?

Buy "No ads" (or the Starter pack) in the Shop, under Bundles. Ads between levels stop; ads you choose to watch for rewards stay available.

How do I restore my purchases on a new phone?

Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Is my progress saved if I change phones?

Progress is stored on your phone only, so it doesn't move to a new phone yet. Purchases can be restored (see above).

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Settings → Daily reminder. If it doesn't turn on, allow notifications for Gears in your phone's settings.
