Privacy Policy

Scorro

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Scorro is a score keeper for tennis, padel and pickleball. It is built to work without an account and collects as little as possible.

Data that stays on your devices

Matches you score, your match history, player names, settings and the format you last used are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. They are not sent to us.

Shared matches

When you start or join a shared match, the app signs in to our backend (Supabase, hosted in the EU) with an anonymous account. No email, phone number or password is involved. For that match we store:

  • a random account identifier,
  • the name you enter for yourself,
  • the match format, player names and the points scored.

Only the people who joined that match with its code can read it. We use this data only to keep the match in sync between phones. We do not sell it, share it with third parties for advertising, or use it to track you.

Purchases

Scorro Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is active.

Analytics and tracking

The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Deleting your data

Local data is removed when you delete the app. To have shared match data removed from our backend, email us and include the match code if you have it.
